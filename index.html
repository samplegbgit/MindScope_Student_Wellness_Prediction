    03 / TRANSPARENCY

    About the model

    Understand what this prototype can and cannot tell you.

    01

    Model output

    The score is returned by your trained machine-learning model through the FastAPI prediction endpoint.

    02

    Evaluation metrics

    Accuracy, MAE, RMSE, R² and validation details have not been supplied to this interface. Add the actual training results before publishing model-performance claims.

    03

    Limitations

    A prediction is not a diagnosis. The result may be affected by training-data quality, bias, missing context and differences between people.

    ⓘ

    Important: The model's score range and the meaning of higher or lower values must be documented from the training pipeline. Until then, MindScope displays the numeric output without classifying it as good or bad.
